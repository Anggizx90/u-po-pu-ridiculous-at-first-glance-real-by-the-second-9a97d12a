A [make.fun](https://make.fun/c/u-po-pu-ridiculous-at-first-glance-real-by-the-second-9a97d12a) bounty entry for **Ridiculous at First Glance, Real by the Second**: a program that writes one sentence, at most 200 characters, pitching a startup idea so strange it sounds like a joke — until the business logic lands.

## The pitch

> We run funerals for houseplants: $29 buys owners who named their fern a tiny coffin, a eulogy and a dignified composting, because nobody throws a family member in the kitchen bin.

179 characters, one sentence. The absurdity is the product itself — a funeral, with coffin, eulogy and composting, for a houseplant — and the business is real on its face: pet death-care is a proven multi-billion market built on exactly this guilt and attachment, and nobody has claimed the neighbouring shelf. The sentence names the thing sold, the price, and the buyer (owners who named their fern), and turns on the last clause: a named plant is family, and family does not go in the kitchen bin.

The full candidate pool (14 pitches, scored against the judge's five criteria in order) and the head-to-head reasoning are in [NOTES.md](NOTES.md).

## How to run

```sh
node main.ts
```

This writes the pitch to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`). No dependencies, no network.

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out node main.ts && cat /tmp/out/entry.txt
```
